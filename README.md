# One Piece

A Japanese manga and anime series created by **Eiichiro Oda**.  
It follows **Monkey D. Luffy** and his crew, the Straw Hat Pirates, on their adventure across the seas to find the legendary treasure known as the *One Piece* and make Luffy the Pirate King.

## Main Characters

- **Monkey D. Luffy** – Rubber-powered captain who dreams of becoming the Pirate King  
- **Roronoa Zoro** – Swordsman aiming to become the world’s greatest  
- **Nami** – Skilled navigator and cartographer  
- **Usopp** – Inventive sharpshooter and storyteller  
- **Sanji** – Chef who fights with powerful kicks  
- **Tony Tony Chopper** – Reindeer doctor with transformation powers  
- **Nico Robin** – Archaeologist seeking the true history  
- **Franky** – Cyborg shipwright who built the Thousand Sunny  
- **Brook** – Skeleton musician revived by the Revive-Revive Fruit  
- **Jinbe** – Fish-man helmsman and former Warlord of the Sea  

---

## Devil Fruits

**Devil Fruits** grant unique abilities but take away the user’s ability to swim.

**Types:**
1. **Paramecia** – Gives superhuman abilities  
2. **Zoan** – Allows transformation into animals  
3. **Logia** – Grants control over natural elements  

Example:  
`Luffy ate the Gomu Gomu no Mi, which made his body stretch like rubber.`
